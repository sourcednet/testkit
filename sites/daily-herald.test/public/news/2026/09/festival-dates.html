Harvest festival moves to October

The annual harvest festival will move from late September to the second weekend of October this year, organizers said on Tuesday. The change gives farmers from the upper valley more time to bring in their crops before the market opens, which many had asked for after last year's early frost.

The festival will again fill the main square and the riverside park, with stalls from more than eighty producers, a craft market, and music on two stages. Entry remains free, and the organizers plan to run extra buses from the villages on both days.
